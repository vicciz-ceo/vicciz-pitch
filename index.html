vicciz · Trip care for travel agencies

The AI agent that re-coordinates the whole trip when travel breaks

A flight delay can unravel the hotel check-in, transfer and tour. Kiipling brings those bookings into one case, prepares requests to each provider, and keeps the agency in control.

vicciz is a travel-technology company building Kiipling, an AI agent for online travel agencies, travel agents and tour operators. It connects a flight disruption to the other bookings it affects, prepares messages to the relevant providers, and tracks their replies in the same case.

The service is designed for trip care under the agency's own brand. The traveller or agency operator reviews each proposed message before it is sent over WhatsApp or email. Booking changes depend on provider confirmation; cases that need a person can be handed to an operator. Talk to us about an agency pilot.

The problem

A trip is assembled from many vendors. It breaks as one.

A traveller books the flight through one site, the hotel through another, the transfer through a third and the tour directly with an operator. A two-hour flight delay can put the transfer outside its pickup window, the hotel past check-in and the tour out of reach.

Rebooking the flight leaves those arrangements to coordinate. Someone still has to work out what changed, contact each provider and keep the traveller informed. For an agency, one disruption can become several conversations about the same trip.

The product

Coordinate the bookings affected by the same disruption

Cross-vendor incident coordination

A delayed flight, late hotel check-in and missed transfer belong to one case. Kiipling identifies which pickup window moved, which check-in needs attention and which tour is now unreachable, so the agency can coordinate the next steps across providers.

Provider outreach without provider API integrations

Kiipling prepares requests over WhatsApp and email for hotels, transfer companies and tour operators. It can reach providers that have no booking API, including those booked elsewhere. Travel businesses connect their trip data through an API; individual providers do not need a technical integration.

How it works

From a flight disruption to a coordinated response

  1. 1

    Monitor

    Check flight-status signals against the booked itinerary to identify delays and cancellations that affect the trip.

  2. 2

    Identify affected bookings

    Connect the new arrival time to hotel check-in, transfer pickup and activity bookings. Prioritise the requests that need attention soonest.

  3. 3

    Prepare provider requests

    Draft a specific message for each affected provider, using booking details, available provider terms and relevant passenger-rights rules such as EU261 and US DOT regulations.

  4. 4

    Review and approve

    The traveller or agency operator sees the proposed message and approves each action before it is sent.

  5. 5

    Track replies and outcomes

    Send approved requests over WhatsApp and email, track provider replies in the same case, and surface unresolved items for an operator. Booking changes depend on provider confirmation.

Pilot focus

Prove the outcome on real disrupted trips

The first white-label pilot focuses on small agencies running Kivrey Tsadikim pilgrimage tours: coordinating flight disruptions with hotel and ground-transport bookings, with operator takeover available. The pilot needs to establish four outcomes.

Confirmed resolution

Measure how many cases reach a confirmed outcome with the affected providers, and record which requests remain unresolved.

Operator effort

Track the time an agency spends reviewing requests, following up and taking over cases that need human judgement.

Time to recovery

Measure the time from detecting a disruption to a confirmed outcome, including how long providers take to respond.

Cost per resolved case

Include messaging, service costs and operator time, then compare the result with the agency's own cost of handling similar cases.

Why now

AI can prepare the coordination work. Agencies keep control.

Reading booking confirmations, connecting a changed arrival time to other reservations and drafting provider messages are tasks an AI agent can bring into one workflow. That gives agencies a way to evaluate automation on a complete case, including the work left for their operators.

The opportunity is to reduce the time spent piecing a trip back together while keeping review, approval and escalation in the agency's hands. Pilots will test how much of that work Kiipling can take on in practice.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is vicciz?

vicciz is a travel-technology company building Kiipling, an AI agent for travel agencies coordinating disrupted trips. Kiipling identifies affected bookings, prepares provider requests and tracks replies across hotels, transfers, tours and activities.

What is the difference between vicciz and Kiipling?

vicciz is the company and consumer brand. Kiipling is the AI trip agent it builds for white-label trip care.

How does Kiipling work alongside flight-rebooking tools?

Rebooking a flight can leave a hotel check-in, transfer pickup or tour needing attention. Kiipling coordinates requests to those providers, including bookings made elsewhere. Changes depend on each provider's confirmation.

Does Kiipling act without asking the traveller?

Every provider-facing action requires approval from the traveller or agency operator before it is sent. They can review the proposed message and take over cases that need a person.

Does a travel business need to integrate anything?

Travel businesses connect trip data through an API. Hotels, transfer companies and tour operators do not need their own API integration: Kiipling contacts them over WhatsApp and email.

What does it cost?

Target pricing is $8–15 per resolved incident, to be validated in pilots. Pricing, commercial terms and the definition of a resolved incident are agreed with each travel business.

Which channels does the agent use?

Provider outreach uses WhatsApp and email. A voice channel is planned. Channel setup and availability are agreed for each pilot.

Where are the application and database hosted?

Application services run on Google Cloud Run in europe-west3 (Frankfurt), with PostgreSQL persistence in eu-central-1. Both are EU regions.

Contact hello@vicciz.com to discuss an agency pilot. We agree the supported trip types, provider channels, operator handoff and success measures with each partner. Contact us about traveller access and supported trip types.
